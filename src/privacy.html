Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

Volt Runner is a free browser game run by Ben Gary Leo G (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what personal data the game handles, why, who else can see it, and the choices you have. You can play as a guest without giving me any personal data.

The short version

  • Guest: nothing about you is sent to me. Your best score stays on your own device.
  • Account: I store your email, a username, your password (scrambled so nobody can read it) and your best score.
  • Public: only your username and best score appear on the leaderboard. Your email is never shown.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and I never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account yourself, any time, from the game menu.

2. What is stored on your own device

The game does not use cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage, which the game needs in order to work:

  • Your best score as a guest, and your sound and joystick settings.
  • If you are logged in: your login session, your last known best score, and any new best score that is waiting to be sent because you were offline.
  • An offline copy of the game itself, so it can start without internet.

Clearing your browser’s site data removes all of this.

3. No advertising, analytics or tracking

The game has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools. Fonts and all game code are served from the game’s own website, so your browser does not contact other companies just to show the game (it only contacts Supabase when you log in, view the leaderboard or save a score).

4. The public leaderboard

Your username and best score are public. Anyone who opens the game can see them. Choose a username that does not reveal who you are.

5. Who handles your data

  • Supabase runs the accounts and database. My project is hosted in Tokyo, Japan.
  • Vercel hosts the website (United States and a global network of servers).

They process data on my behalf and under their own terms and privacy policies. I do not sell your personal data and I do not share it for advertising. I may disclose data if the law requires it. Because these providers are in other countries, your data may be processed outside the country where you live; I use providers that offer data-processing terms covering this.

6. My legal reasons (for people in the EU and UK)

  • Contract: providing your account, your score and the leaderboard.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the game secure and stopping cheating and abuse.

7. How long I keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Provider server logs are kept for as long as Vercel and Supabase keep them under their own policies. Data on your device stays there until you clear it.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or California law) you can ask to see the data I hold about you, correct it, delete it, receive a copy, or object to or restrict how it is used.

  • Delete your account yourself: log in, then use “Delete my account” on the main menu. This permanently erases your email, username, score and run records.
  • Anything else: email me at the address below. I will reply within 30 days.
  • You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
  • I do not “sell” or “share” personal information as those terms are used in California law.

9. Children

You must be at least 13 to create an account. If you live in the EU or UK and your country sets a higher age for online consent (up to 16), you need your parent or guardian’s permission. Younger players can still play as a guest, which sends no personal data to me. If I learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, I will delete it. Parents can email me to ask for a child’s data to be removed.

10. Security

The game uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS), passwords are stored only as scrambled hashes, and database rules stop players from reading other people’s private data. No system is perfectly secure, but I take reasonable care, and if a breach affects you I will tell you as the law requires.

11. Changes to this policy

If I change how the game handles data I will update this page and its date. For important changes I will also show a notice in the game.
